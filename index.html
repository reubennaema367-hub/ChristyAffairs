About Christy Affairs

Christy Affairs is a beauty-focused hair business serving customers around Ada George and Port Harcourt. We ventilate, make, color, fit and restore wigs, and we teach the craft to others.

We care about beauty, confidence and the small details: the hairline, the lace, the fit and the finish. Our aim is a professional, elegant result that you feel good wearing.
